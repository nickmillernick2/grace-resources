# Jesus's Teachings on Prayer

Compiled from the Gospels. Use these references to build prayer resources.

## The Lord's Prayer (The Model)
- **Matthew 6:9-13** — The foundational prayer Jesus taught
- **Luke 11:2-4** — Luke's version (slightly different phrasing)

## Ask, Seek, Knock
- **Matthew 7:7-11** — "Ask and it will be given to you; seek and you will find; knock and the door will be opened"
- **Luke 11:9-13** — Similar passage with emphasis on God's generosity

## Praying in Jesus's Name
- **John 14:13-14** — "Whatever you ask in my name, I will do it"
- **John 15:16** — "You did not choose me, but I chose you... so that whatever you ask the Father in my name, he will give you"
- **John 16:23-24** — "My Father will give you whatever you ask in my name"

## Faith in Prayer
- **Mark 11:24** — "Believe that you have received it, and it will be yours"
- **Matthew 21:22** — "If you believe, you will receive whatever you ask for in prayer"

## Persistence & Confidence in Prayer
- **Luke 18:1-8** — The parable of the persistent widow
- **1 John 5:14-15** — "This is the confidence we have in approaching God: that if we ask anything according to his will, he hears us"

## Forgiveness in Prayer
- **Mark 11:25-26** — "When you stand praying, if you hold anything against anyone, forgive them"
- **Matthew 5:23-24** — Reconciliation before bringing offerings to God

## Private Prayer / Not for Show
- **Matthew 6:5-6** — "When you pray, do not be like the hypocrites... But when you pray, go into your room, close the door"

## Praying According to God's Will
- **Matthew 26:39** — Jesus's prayer in Gethsemane: "My Father, if it is possible, may this cup be taken from me. Yet not as I will, but as you will"
- **1 John 5:14** — "If we ask anything according to his will, he hears us"

## Watchfulness & Constant Prayer
- **Matthew 26:41** — "Watch and pray so that you will not fall into temptation"
- **Luke 21:36** — "Be always on the watch, and pray"

## Corporate/Community Prayer
- **Matthew 18:19-20** — "If two of you on earth agree about anything they ask for, it will be done for them by my Father in heaven. For where two or three gather in my name, there am I with them"

## Summary Themes
1. **Simplicity** — Pray honestly, without fancy words
2. **Faith** — Believe God will answer
3. **Persistence** — Keep asking, don't give up
4. **Forgiveness** — Let go of bitterness before you pray
5. **God's Will** — Align your desires with His, not the other way around
6. **Community** — Pray together with others
7. **Humility** — Pray in secret, not to impress people
